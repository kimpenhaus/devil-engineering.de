Overview

This website is a static site. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools and has no contact forms. Personal data is only processed in the cases described below.

Hosting on GitHub Pages

This website is served via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you visit the website, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, the date and time of access, the page requested and information about your browser and operating system. GitHub stores this data in server log files to deliver the website and keep it secure, for example to fend off attacks. I have no access to these log files.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. My legitimate interest lies in providing the website securely, reliably and economically.

Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, so the transfer is covered by an adequacy decision of the European Commission under Art. 45 GDPR. GitHub determines how long it keeps the data: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Contacting me

If you contact me by e-mail, phone or LinkedIn, I process the information in your message to handle your request. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR if your request concerns a contract or steps prior to one, otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is answering requests. I delete the data once the request has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations, for example under commercial or tax law, require otherwise.

Links to LinkedIn and GitHub

The links to LinkedIn and GitHub are plain links. Data only reaches the provider once you click a link; its own privacy policy applies there.
